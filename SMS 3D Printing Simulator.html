PrintLab 3D Printing Game

10 levels, 10 customer orders. Design it, verify it, slice it, print it!

👋

Welcome to PrintLab, the 3D printing game! You run a print shop: customers send orders, and you turn each one into a real printed model.

  • 🎮 10 levels, each with a new customer order
  • 🪜 Every level has 10 stages: design, verify, slice, supports, filament, prepare, print, collect, export
  • ⭐ Earn up to 3 stars per level. Fewer mistakes mean more points
  • 💡 Tip: drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom, Ctrl+A selects all shapes
